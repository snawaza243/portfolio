My Story

Growing up in Siwan, Bihar, I didn't have access to tech communities, meetups, or mentors. What I had was curiosity and an internet connection. I became fascinated not with what software looked like — but with how it worked at the infrastructure level.

After graduating with an 8.31 CGPA in Computer Science from MM University, I landed a software internship at Vienna Advantage in Mohali — and shipped production code in my first four weeks. That early exposure wired a mindset into me: own what you build, and make it production-grade.

Today at Blu Parrot Venture in Gurgaon, I've architected entire backend systems from scratch, migrated legacy financial platforms off VB6, and maintained 20+ business-critical modules for enterprise clients. I wasn't handed a role — I expanded it. I became the sole backend SPOC because I treated every system like it was mine to protect.

I'm not a generalist who does a bit of everything. I'm a backend-specialist who happens to know how the full stack connects.
